# CAPTURE — Product Requirements Document

## Product Thesis

> **Turn more of the demand you already have into revenue.**

Capture is a productised revenue-execution company.

It helps established businesses identify and act on revenue opportunities already sitting inside their customer demand, conversations, history and data.

The product does not primarily sell AI, automation, WhatsApp management or chatbots.

Those are mechanisms.

The customer is buying:

> **More of the demand they already have turning into money.**

---

# 1. Strategic Positioning

## The problem

Businesses spend significant amounts of money and effort generating customer demand.

But revenue leaks occur between:

**Demand → Conversation → Follow-up → Conversion → Repeat Revenue**

Examples include:

- enquiries that receive no response
- delayed responses
- customers who show buying intent but disappear
- quotes that are never followed up
- leads that become dormant
- previous customers who are never reactivated
- conversations that require human intervention but are missed
- sales teams that cannot consistently follow through

The business may therefore possess substantial **unrealised revenue** without knowing where it is or how much it represents.

## Capture's role

Capture sits between:

# **Customer Demand → Business Revenue**

Its job is to identify leakage, recover existing opportunities and prevent new leakage.

The strategic principle is:

> **The channels will change. The problem won't.**

Today that may mean:

**WhatsApp + Instagram → lost enquiries → lost revenue**

Tomorrow it may include:

- email
- websites
- CRM
- calls
- SMS
- new messaging platforms
- future AI channels

Capture should therefore not become a channel-specific AI product.

# 4. Product Architecture

Capture has three commercial products.

# FIND

### Revenue Leak Audit

Free diagnostic.

Purpose:

> **Identify where the business is losing money in existing customer conversations.**

---

# RECOVER

### Revenue Recovery

One-time/on-demand recovery campaign.

Purpose:

> **Recover dormant leads, customers and opportunities that have already gone cold.**

Recover replaces the previous working name **Rebound**.

### Important commercial principle

Recover is **not inherently recurring**.

A business may have a finite backlog of dormant opportunities.

Once that backlog has been worked, Recover may not have the same job to perform every month, particularly if Prevent is active.

Therefore:

> **Recover = on-demand revenue recovery campaign.**

A customer can run another campaign later when another meaningful dormant cohort accumulates.

---

# PREVENT

### Revenue Prevention / Response Engine

Recurring product.

Purpose:

> **Prevent new customer demand from going cold.**

Prevent handles:

- instant response
- approved/common questions
- intent identification
- qualification
- human escalation
- controlled follow-up
- outcome measurement

The previous product name "Response" is retired as the primary product name.

"Response" can still be used descriptively for the response capability.

---

# 5. Product Relationship

The three products are not independent products.

They form a revenue lifecycle:

# **FIND → RECOVER → PREVENT**

### Find

Find the money.

### Recover

Recover the money.

### Prevent

Stop losing the money again.

The strategic relationship is:

> **Recover cleans the backlog. Prevent stops the backlog from returning.**

This is one of the core commercial insights behind Capture.

---

# 6. Commercial Funnel

The typical customer journey should be:

### Step 1 — Find

Analyse a sample of customer conversations.

Show the business where revenue leakage exists.

### Step 2 — Recover

Use Recover to act on the most valuable dormant opportunities.

### Step 3 — Prevent

Deploy Prevent so the same leakage patterns do not continue.

The sales narrative is therefore:

> **Find what you're losing → recover what you can → prevent future leakage.**

# 7. FIND — Revenue Leak Audit

## Purpose

The audit is Capture's free diagnostic wedge.

It serves four purposes:

- lead generation
- diagnosis
- qualification
- product recommendation

It should demonstrate that the business has meaningful revenue leakage before asking the customer to buy anything.

## V1 Workflow

The lowest-friction initial experience:

1. Customer lands on Capture website.
2. Selects **Find My Revenue Leaks — Free**.
3. Provides basic business information.
4. Uploads approximately 20–50 representative WhatsApp conversations.
5. Capture analyses them.
6. Customer receives a Revenue Leak Report.

The native WhatsApp export process can be used initially.

Long term, Capture should eliminate manual export requirements.

---

# 8. Audit Information Collection

Keep qualification short.

Collect:

- business name
- industry
- website / Instagram
- approximate annual revenue
- approximate monthly WhatsApp conversations

Avoid creating a long application form.

# 10. Audit Output

The customer receives a:

# Revenue Leak Report

Example:

**47 conversations analysed**

Found:

- 7 unanswered enquiries
- 8 high-intent conversations with no meaningful follow-up
- 4 previous-customer reactivation opportunities
- 19 potentially recoverable conversations

Capture can then estimate potential opportunity value using the customer's average transaction value.

### Critical disclaimer

> **Estimated opportunity value is not a revenue guarantee.**

The report should include anonymised examples where appropriate.

## Revenue Readiness Score

The report may include a simple:

# Capture Revenue Readiness Score

Example:

**64 / 100**

Potential dimensions:

- Response
- Follow-up
- Recovery

This is a communication device, not sophisticated predictive analytics.

# 11. RECOVER — Revenue Recovery

## Purpose

Recover identifies dormant opportunities and re-initiates appropriate conversations.

Potential targets:

### 1. Unanswered enquiry

Customer contacted the business.

Business failed to meaningfully respond.

→ Eligible for recovery.

### 2. Cold high-intent conversation

Customer demonstrated buying intent.

Conversation subsequently ended.

→ Wait appropriate period.

→ Follow up.

### 3. Previous customer

Customer purchased previously.

Relevant period passes.

→ Appropriate reactivation message.

---

# 12. Recover Workflow

**Identify**

↓

**Score opportunity**

↓

**Determine appropriate timing**

↓

**Send personalised follow-up**

↓

If customer responds:

**Stop automation**

↓

**Human/business takes over**

If no response:

**One further follow-up where appropriate**

↓

**Stop**

Core principle:

> **Recover opens the door. It does not conduct an endless AI conversation.**

Avoid spam and excessive follow-up.

# 14. Recover Attribution

V1 attribution should remain deliberately simple.

A conversation becomes **Recovered** when:

1. Capture initiated the reactivation.
2. Customer responded positively.
3. Business records a resulting sale.

Business can mark:

- Won
- Lost
- Not sure

Revenue can initially be entered manually.

Do not build sophisticated attribution before proving the commercial model.

Reason:

> **Capture should come to the sales team, not force the sales team to come to Capture.**

---

# 21. Human Routing

V1 can support simple routing:

- all leads → sales manager
- round-robin assignment
- product → salesperson

Avoid sophisticated territory routing initially.

---

# 22. Escalation Timers

Example:

### T+0

AI hands off.

### T+10 minutes

No human action:

→ salesperson reminder.

### T+30 minutes

Still no action:

→ manager escalation.

This turns human follow-through into an explicit system rather than relying on someone remembering to check a dashboard.

---

# 23. Human State Control

Do not attempt to infer whether a human's response was "good enough."

Use explicit states.

Notification can provide:

- Take Conversation
- Assign
- Dismiss

When salesperson selects:

### Take Conversation

AI automation stops.

This is a critical trust feature.

---

# 24. Prevent Follow-Through

Prevent should not merely answer questions.

Example:

Customer:

> How much?

AI responds with approved pricing and a useful next step.

Customer:

> I'll think about it.

Prevent may then initiate:

→ one appropriate follow-up

→ potentially one further follow-up

→ stop.

Therefore:

> **Prevent = response + qualification + controlled follow-through + human escalation**

Not merely chatbot functionality.

- channel access is available
- business information is complete

- there is no unusual workflow complexity

# 29. Permanent-Fix Principle

Capture should minimise recurring intervention.

> **If a problem can be solved permanently, Capture should solve it permanently rather than manufacture recurring usage.**

This principle is particularly important for Recover.

Capture should not artificially create monthly recovery work merely to preserve subscription revenue.

The business model should follow genuine customer value.

# 30. Shared Capture Engine

Find, Recover and Prevent should not be three unrelated systems.

They should use the same underlying revenue-execution engine.

## CAPTURE ENGINE

### 1. Capture

Receive conversation/customer data.

↓

### 2. Understand

Classify the conversation.

↓

### 3. Store

Create customer and opportunity records.

↓

### 4. Trigger

Rules determine the next action.

↓

### 5. Act

Send message or notification.

↓

### 6. Handoff

Human takes over where required.

↓

### 7. Measure

Record outcome and revenue.

This shared engine is the long-term defensibility opportunity.

The individual features can be copied.

The accumulated system for **understanding demand → deciding action → executing → measuring revenue** is harder to replicate.

# 33. Security & Data Protection

Capture processes customer communications.

V1 must include:

- encrypted transmission
- encrypted storage
- access controls
- customer authorisation
- defined retention period
- deletion process
- access audit where feasible
- no training of models on customer conversations without appropriate explicit permission
- appropriate privacy/data-processing terms
- minimisation of personal data sent to external AI providers

Nigeria's data protection requirements should be considered from the beginning.

# 42. V1 Build Philosophy

## Manual before automated

Validate workflows manually before automating them.

## Rules before intelligence

Use deterministic rules wherever possible.

## One channel before many

WhatsApp first for Recover.

WhatsApp + Instagram for Prevent where technically reliable.

## Human fallback everywhere

AI should fail safely.

## Revenue before sophistication

A simple system recovering ₦2m is more valuable than a sophisticated AI demonstration.

## Productise repeated work

Anything repeatedly done manually should eventually become configuration/software.
